ResQVision

Find the damage.
Send teams where it matters first.

Give it a satellite image from before and one from after. It marks the damaged buildings and blocked roads, ranks them, and hands your team a list with GPS locations and a route.

The demo is a generated town with known damage. It shows the whole workflow in a few seconds.

    One inspection team

      Open in Google Maps

      Most urgent site first, then the nearest next. A quick, explainable order, not a guaranteed shortest tour.

      The satellite basemap comes from Esri and loads from the internet in your browser. Click a site for its GPS location and a Google Maps link.

      Confidence is a heuristic, based on the distance to the nearest severity threshold and on how well the images aligned. It is not a calibrated probability.
